What Causes Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying can have many different causes, and its effects can impact people both online and in their everyday lives. Understanding these causes and effects can help us recognize cyberbullying and work toward preventing it.

Abominity

People hide behind screens and feel they won't get caught

Peer Presure

Wanting to fit in or impress others can lead to hurtful behavior.

Personal Issues

Sress, anger, family problems or low self-esteem can trigger it..
