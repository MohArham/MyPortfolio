What is Cyberbullying?

Imagine opening your phone and finding hurtful messages, embarrassing photos, or cruel comments waiting for you. This is cyberbullying, a serious problem that affects millions of children and teenagers around the world.

Where it happens

It happens through social media, online games, messaging apps, and other digital platforms, making it difficult for victims to escape

Why it matters

Cyberbullying can damage a person's confidence, mental health, relationships, and even their ability to learn. As technology keeps evolving, understanding cyberbullying and learning how to prevent it is more important than ever.

You can help

By raising awareness and encouraging kindness online, we can help create a safer digital world for everyone, without making anyone feel hurt.

Let's build a community where everyone feels safe and respected. By lifting each other up and embracing our differences, we can create an inclusive space built on mutual trust, kindness, and shared growth for every single member.
