How to prevent CyberBullying

We all have the power to make children, teenagers and adults feel safe when using the internet.

BLOCK

It happens through social media, online games, messaging apps, and other digital platforms, making it difficult for victims to escape.

REPORT

Cyberbullying can damage a person's confidence, mental health, relationships, and even their ability to learn. As technology keeps evolving, understanding cyberbullying and learning how to prevent it is more important than ever.

TELL

By raising awareness and encouraging kindness online, we can help create a safer digital world for everyone, without making anyone feel hurt.
